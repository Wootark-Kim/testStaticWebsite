Group Project: SAVNAC

Project Description

Savnac is a somewhat faithful recreation of the canvas.instructure.com (where teachers can create their own courses) gradebook system. There are two types of users: Students and Teachers. Anyone can create either a Teacher account or a Student account. Teachers have the ability to create courses and assignments in said courses, and Students can enroll themselves into courses that Teachers create. There is no limitation on how many courses a Teacher can create, and there is no limitation on how many courses a Student can join.
